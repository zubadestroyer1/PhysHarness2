each. The strict schema, target-only export and evaluator packaging are implemented in
`physharness.science.physics_benchmarks` and `benchmark_artifacts`.

sources and known library shortcuts are public, so family holdouts do not establish
pretraining independence or secret evaluation data. These are reproducible capability
tasks, not claims of novel physics or open-problem difficulty.
