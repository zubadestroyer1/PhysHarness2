The elapsed time and cost after first proof include this helper's work. Do not infer a general inefficiency or a benefit of either sharing policy from one run. Compare first-proof time/cost separately from whole-attempt time/cost, and examine actual deliveries and messages in later `ideas` runs before attributing any difference to collaboration.
