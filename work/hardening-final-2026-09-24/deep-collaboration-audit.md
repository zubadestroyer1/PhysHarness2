# Collaboration and scheduling audit of the physics proof pilots

Read-only forensic audit, 2026-09-24/25 UTC. Primary evidence is the immutable `export/manifest.json` and hash-named blobs, plus read-only SQLite event logs (`harness.db`, opened with `mode=ro`) for the calibration, first cooperative attempt, and fresh cooperative retry. The retry export reports snapshot event sequence 6832 and manifest SHA-256 `81d1b15080d4fd7db1f13a266c8b5d4c2b4e9131bee942bbbe70aaffe9236221`. Existing live-audit notes were used as orientation, not as substitutes for those records. This report summarizes visible objectives, messages, artifact metadata/content overlap, delivery acknowledgments, and lifecycle events; it does not expose private reasoning or credentials. An acknowledgment shows the reader fetched a delivery batch, not that the reader adopted every idea in it. Source-line overlap establishes reuse, not a causal counterfactual.

## Main findings

1. **The two nominally independent roots converged on the same proof architecture almost immediately.** Both were seeded with the same free-form objective, `Investigate the reviewed target; choose methods and collaborators freely.` In the retry, root 1's algebra helper sent the perturbed-energy construction at 23:51:43 and its corrected sharp constant at 23:51:50. Root 2 sent the same `L = E + ε∑m qv + (ε/2)∑γq²` construction and target constants to its inequality helper at 23:51:52; the algebra helper sent the construction across to root 2 at 23:51:56. These are early attributed messages, before formal proof acceptance. The roots subsequently divided labor effectively, but the run gives little evidence of sustained *strategy* diversity. Their independent value was in Lean implementation and checking: root 1 supplied a checked scalar `weighted_decay` source artifact `d5595842` at 23:54:54, while root 2 assembled and submitted the accepted target source.

2. **Queueing made one helper's original assignment stale.** Four worker slots were occupied by two roots and the first two helpers by 23:51:24. Root 2 queued a comparison-integration helper `5a94d5ae` at 23:51:28, and a pointwise-inequality helper `30fccdf6` at 23:51:35. The inequality helper leased only at 00:01:27 (9m52s queued); the comparison helper leased only at 00:03:11 (11m43s queued). Root 1 had already shared a Lean-checked scalar decay artifact `d5595842` at 23:54:58, and root 2 explicitly told its queued comparison helper at 23:57:56 that the lemma existed and to pivot if scheduled. The helper finally acknowledged that artifact and pivot at 00:03:19. This is observed adaptation, but its initial objective and long queue did not cause an automatic reassignment or cancellation. The helper spent roughly two minutes reviewing already assembled comparison/dissipation and sending a small assembly artifact, while root 2 reported a full locally compiling theorem at 00:05:18. The records do not prove this helper's work was useless; they do show that the task definition was obsolete before it ran.

3. **Duplicate lemma work occurred despite direct messaging.** Root 1 shared the scalar decay proof at 23:54:58. Its own algebra helper then produced another checked `differential_decay` artifact `d7bf2f3f` at 23:59:08 and sent it to both roots. Root 2's delayed comparison helper had been assigned the same topic. Quadratic-form differentiation was also checked independently by root 2's derivative helper (`d46bf931`, 23:53:40), root 1's algebra helper (`1107b256`, 00:00:39), and root 2's pointwise helper (`8f33ab32`, 00:07:08), the last after root 2's full local proof. This duplication may have provided validation, but no explicit “second implementation for review” objective or stop rule is recorded. It consumed scarce four-slot capacity while the full derivative and assembly path were critical.

4. **The useful collaboration is traceable through artifacts, not only message claims.** Root 2's accepted `lean_source` artifact `a61e050f` (27,321 bytes, SHA-256 prefix `d54ff7f2`) was submitted at 00:07:36 and independently verified at 00:08:14 by receipt `9c834332` (`kernel_checked`, `independent_kernel`). Hash-named export blobs show the accepted source contains all 22 substantive normalized lines of root 1's `weighted_decay` artifact `d5595842`; all 60/60 of root 2's early comparison artifact `afa8dd7a`; all 61/61 of root 2's dissipation artifact `79fcab46`; and the derivative helper's substantive lines in `690fd188` (62/62), `692601d3` (23/23), and `80c9bc4e` (58/58), with 31/32 from the earlier `d46bf931`. The source comparison removes whitespace and comment-only lines and excludes very short lines; it is a descriptive overlap measure, not semantic proof. The independent receipt is the acceptance evidence. A prior live audit also matched root 2's uploaded `scratch/DerivativePieces.lean` to helper `d46bf931` and observed subsequent zero-exit local Lean runs. This combination establishes actual component transfer and reuse.

5. **The direct channel worked, but delivery accounting must not be mistaken for adoption or a general discussion bus.** The retry contains 60 direct `message` records, 47 acknowledged `discussion_delivery` batches, and zero `discussion_post`, `discussion_topic`, or subscription records. One batch can contain multiple messages: 57 of 60 direct messages appear in acknowledged delivery items. Of root 2's 29 inbound messages, 28 were acknowledged; root 1 acknowledged all 18 inbound messages. The three unacknowledged retry messages were a late 00:09:07 follow-up to root 2, a 00:03:39 message to the algebra helper, and a post-verification 00:08:37 thanks to the derivative helper. The algebra helper's single inbound message was not acknowledged, so it should not be credited as consumed. The zero discussion-post count means this pilot exercised direct messages and fetched delivery batches, not a topic-based discussion bus.

6. **The first cooperative attempt had a much larger dead-recipient problem.** Root 1 `a4666609` blocked at 23:10:20 after the `/work` path mistake; it had only three acknowledged inbound messages, while 24 of its 27 inbound messages were never acknowledged. Many were created well after the block, including at 23:30:16 and 23:30:28 near or after independent verification. The second root's 17 inbound messages were all acknowledged. A helper `46e5908f` blocked at 23:14:55 from another path failure; its late 23:24:13 inbound message was unacknowledged. These unacknowledged messages are successful *sends*, not successful knowledge transfer. The surviving root did reuse other helper source and obtained a verified receipt at 23:30:11, but the first attempt's blocked workflow is a separate lifecycle result.

7. **There was no proof-triggered early-stop policy in the retry.** Root 2 reported locally compiling full source at 00:05:18, submitted at 00:07:36, and received the independent verified receipt at 00:08:14. At the receipt, both roots and the pointwise helper were still active; root 2 completed at 00:09:01, the helper at 00:09:16, and root 1 at 00:09:35. After the receipt, 27 `resources.settled` events totaling $6.284783 were recorded. Of these, 21 were model response settlements; a separate request/start reconciliation found 18 model requests actually started after acceptance, totaling $5.410400. These are timing measures, **not** proof of avoidable spend: some work was useful wrap-up, confirmation, or notification, and some settlements came from pre-receipt starts. The task timeline and post-receipt follow-ups do show no immediate verified-claim cancellation or drain of outstanding workers. The retry's final settled total was $49.102475. The 00:08:30 notification from root 2 to root 1 was acknowledged by root 1 before its 00:09:35 completion; root 1's final continuation was therefore informed by the verified result.

8. **Final helper reports could lag the global scientific status.** Root 2's pointwise helper finished at 00:09:16 saying its parent had reported verification but the helper could not access the private receipt, so it explicitly withheld independent confirmation. This was appropriately cautious. The earlier comparison helper's 00:05:15 final report still said full-target verification remained, which was true at its completion. Root 1's final report did cite the verified receipt and full source after acknowledging root 2's notification. Status summaries should therefore be interpreted at their task completion times and evidence access levels, rather than compared as if all branches had a synchronized global view.

## Practical changes to evaluate

- At each helper lease, deliver a compact current-state brief and require an explicit **continue / pivot / cancel** decision against already checked artifacts and the current bottleneck. Store that decision as a task event. The 9–12 minute waits make spawn-time objectives unreliable.
- Track work claims by lemma or interface (for example `quadratic_deriv`, scalar decay, finite comparison), with an owner, artifact ID, local-check status, and consumer. When a second worker claims the same component, record whether it is intentional independent review. This would expose the scalar-decay and quadratic-derivative duplication without suppressing useful cross-checks.
- Make delivery reports distinguish sent, fetched/acknowledged, imported into a workspace, locally checked, and included in a submitted proof. The retry has evidence at all of these levels, but raw message counts flatten them. Inactive or blocked recipients should be marked unavailable, and new sends to them should be rejected or rerouted to the surviving root.
- On a verified target receipt, notify every live task and stop or drain work according to an explicit policy, while preserving any in-flight settlement/accounting obligations. Measure requests *started* after acceptance and task-specific costs to quantify savings; settlement timestamps alone are insufficient.
- If future evaluations seek diverse proof strategies, assign distinct approaches or hold cross-root sharing until each root records an initial plan. The present retry should be described as collaborative assembly with two roots, not as evidence that two independent strategies were explored to completion.

## Scope limits

The manifest is an exported snapshot, and its `discussion_delivery` records show acknowledged batches rather than private model comprehension. The report does not infer that any component was mathematically necessary from line overlap alone, that a worker could not have found it independently, or that post-verification settlements were entirely avoidable. The separate verifier receipt establishes target acceptance in the retry and first cooperative run. The calibration's completed task did not establish target acceptance.
