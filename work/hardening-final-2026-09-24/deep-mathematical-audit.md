# Mathematical audit of the recorded oscillator proof

Scope: the immutable exports for calibration, the first cooperative attempt, and the clean cooperative retry. This is an audit of visible messages, exported Lean sources, checkpoint source chunks, workspace-operation records, and the verification record. It does not inspect or reconstruct private model reasoning. All times below are UTC on 24–25 September 2026. Artifact IDs resolve through each attempt's `export/manifest.json` to SHA-256-named bytes in the same directory.

## What the accepted retry actually proves

The clean retry's exact submitted `lean_source` is artifact `a61e050f-70b2-4166-a454-4b1740d5255a`, created 2026-09-25 00:07:36, SHA-256 `d54ff7f271170c2ec50440105e8828a4b3b45fa47b4b267139703c1293b272cf`, 27,321 bytes (606 lines). The manifest's verification record and `retry-final-evaluation.json` bind it to receipt `9c834332-48e9-4e75-947e-9aaa3e5b13b4` and report Comparator, Lean, and Nanoda acceptance of the exact target. The receipt's axiom list is a **permitted policy upper bound**, not a measurement of precisely which axioms the proof used. The submitted source itself has no `sorry`, `admit`, or declared `axiom`. This is a complete formal proof of a known-type energy estimate, not a novelty or publication result.

The central mathematical choice is the corrected energy

`W = E + ε Σᵢ mᵢ qᵢ vᵢ + (ε/2) Σᵢ γᵢ qᵢ²`, with `ε = min(δ/(2M), α/(2Γ))`.

The damping-potential term makes the `γ q v` derivative terms cancel. The source proves `E/2 ≤ W ≤ 2E` by scalar square inequalities, mass/damping bounds, quartic nonnegativity, and coercivity (`pointwise`, lines 12–29; `comparison`, 31–78). It differentiates the quadratic stiffness form using symmetry, separately differentiates kinetic and quartic sums, then uses the ODE to cancel stiffness and cubic terms (`quadratic_deriv`, 234–272; `energy_deriv_ode`, 358–392; `cross_deriv_ode`, 432–476). Finite-sum Young inequalities control `Σfv` and `Σfq`; the resulting bound is `W' ≤ -ε E + (1/δ + ε/(2α)) F²` (`network_dissipation`, 146–194; `final_from_deriv`, 524–533). Since `W ≤ 2E`, an integrating-factor argument gives decay at `ε/2` (`weighted_decay`, 196–225). Converting from W back to E produces exactly the target factor 4 and forcing coefficient `4/(εδ)+2/α` (`bound_from_decay`, 99–119; `final_from_deriv`, 534–548). These constants were an active constraint, not incidental simplification: the first helper's message at 23:51:43 was corrected at 23:51:50 to preserve the undoubled forcing coefficient.

The work is sophisticated Lean integration of an established Lyapunov argument: variable-mass ODE cancellation, symmetric matrix double sums, finite-sum estimates, scalar algebra under division, and all-time differential comparison. The recorded benchmark does not test finding a new physical principle. The target is stronger than necessary in at least two formal respects: `hn : 0 < n` is never referenced in the submitted theorem body (lines 550–606), so this proof also covers the empty finite index type; `hF : 0 ≤ F` is passed through but the decay proof uses `F² ≥ 0` and does not need the sign assumption (`final_from_deriv`, 478–548). This is an inference from the source's explicit dependencies, not a proposed change to the reviewed target. The `δ ≤ Γ` assumption is used in the comparison proof even though positive `n` and the per-index damping bounds imply it mathematically.

## Development detours and blind spots

Local holes were used as scaffolding. In the first cooperative export, checkpoint chunk `446e4114-6ad8-44eb-965f-4395690f413c` at 23:27:05 contains temporary `axiom lyap_finite_comparison` and `axiom networkLyapunov_deriv`; nearby chunks have `sorry`. The first attempt's *submitted* candidate has neither. In the retry, checkpoint chunks `2c983409…` and `b01e953b…` at 23:57:22 leave the symmetry reindexing as `sorry`; `b0f2eecb…` at 00:01:26 leaves an integrating-factor step as `sorry`. Later shareable sources and the submitted candidate close those gaps. The distinction matters: scratch compilation with a hole would not establish theorem completeness, but the final receipt applies to different, hole-free bytes.

The symmetry step was a genuine formal bottleneck: differentiating `ΣᵢΣⱼ Kᵢⱼqᵢqⱼ` produces two double sums, and one needs `Kᵢⱼ=Kⱼᵢ` plus index exchange to collapse them to `2 Σᵢ(ΣⱼKᵢⱼqⱼ)vᵢ`. Multiple branches independently revisited it after other components were done (messages at 00:00:39 and 00:07:08). A second bottleneck was moving from local `lake env lean` success to the exact verifier-bound source: at 00:05:18 the successful root had a full 27,321-byte VM file but lacked a file-path submission API; another root advised `read_workspace_file` and verbatim `submit_candidate` at 00:05:31. The accepted source was stored at 00:07:36, roughly 2m18s after local full compilation. That is workflow overhead, not mathematical search time.

The final theorem's proof is a composition of many private lemmas and `nlinarith`/`ring` closures, so its mathematical narrative is hard to inspect from the last theorem alone. A short explicit proof map and dependency/unused-assumption report would make expert review faster. No visible full-source regression checks explore alternative targets (for example F with negative sign or `n=0`); these would test generality, not challenge the accepted receipt. The available logs show one successful target and do not establish general research capacity or a measured gain from helpers. Some late helper work, such as the 00:07:03 symmetric-quadratic artifact, arrived after full Lean success and was redundant for this candidate.

## Improvements grounded in this run

1. Give each helper a small artifact contract (lemma statement, dependencies, Lean exit result, whether it contains holes) and a coordinator-maintained proof map. This would make direct reuse and redundant late work visible sooner; compare the 23:55:44 request for a forcing bridge, the 00:02:18 bridge delivery, and later duplicate stiffness derivatives.
2. Add a local source scanner that labels any scratch proof using `sorry`/`axiom` as provisional and checks the exact candidate bytes before submission. The first attempt's 23:27 axiom scaffolds and retry's 23:57 holes were valid development devices, but source status was easy to conflate in messages.
3. Allow verified workspace-file-to-artifact submission with exact digest binding. The 00:05:18–00:07:36 manual transfer interval was avoidable and introduced a copying risk after local Lean success.
4. Include an automatically generated theorem dependency summary showing unused assumptions and direct lemma dependencies. Here `hn` and `hF` did not carry mathematical load, and the accepted theorem wrapper hid a substantial derivative and comparison chain.
5. Evaluate future cooperation against a controlled single-root budget and measure artifact consumption, duplicate effort, and time from first local full proof to receipt. These logs prove reuse, not a speedup attributable to four helpers.
